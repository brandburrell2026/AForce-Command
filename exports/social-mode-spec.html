Overview

Social Mode is the subscriber-only "drink smart on nights out" experience inside AForce OS. It tracks per-drink hydration impact in real time, estimates BAC, escalates transportation safety prompts, and runs an 8-hour morning recovery protocol. The voice is calm, protective, never preachy — it never says "safe to drive," never moralizes, and never asserts legal compliance.

3BAC estimation — Widmark approximation

gramsAlcohol = Σ ( drink.oz × ML_PER_OZ × (drink.abv/100) × 0.789 )
bodyMassG    = bodyWeightLbs × 453.592      (floored to 80 lbs)
r            = 0.68 (male / unspecified) | 0.55 (female)
foodFactor   = 0.92 if ateRecently else 1.0
bacRaw       = (gramsAlcohol / (bodyMassG × r)) × 100 × foodFactor
elapsedH     = hours since first drink
bacCurrent   = max(0, bacRaw − 0.015 × elapsedH)

Output: a range, never a single number.

FieldDerivation
rangeLow / rangeHighpoint estimate ± 0.01 (Widmark's ±15–20% literature error)
trendcurrent − BAC 15 min ago; |Δ| < 0.005 = steady, else rising/falling
confidencehigh if sex provided AND ≥50% drinks have explicit oz/abv AND ≤8 drinks; else medium; low once >8 drinks
timeToClearMinutes(bac − 0.005) / 0.015 × 60, rounded up to nearest 5 min (conservative)
notesi18n keys: default_sex, default_strength, food_factored, many_drinks

Constants: ETHANOL_DENSITY = 0.789 g/mL · ML_PER_OZ = 29.5735 · LBS_TO_G = 453.592 · ELIMINATION = 0.015 /h · CLEAR_THRESHOLD = 0.005.

The safety card is hidden below MODERATE so users only see the legal-protection language when it actually applies. The "stop drinking" sub-prompt only appears at HIGH/CRITICAL.

5Recovery Mode

  • Triggered when the user taps End Night in the Social sheet — engine writes socialMode.endedAt.
  • RECOVERY_WINDOW_MS = 8 hours. Rollup flips inRecoveryWindow = true for that window.
  • During recovery, RecoveryModeCard renders:
    1. Estimated time until BAC clears (bac.timeToClearMinutes)
    2. 3-step morning protocol: water → AForce RTD → 7+ hours sleep
    3. Standard disclaimer pair
  • Home banner shifts to amber and reads social.recovery_active.
  • Rollup returns null when Social Mode is neither active nor in recovery window.

6Orb overlay

StatusPulseOrb accepts an optional socialOverlay?: { alcoholLoad: number; unstable: boolean }:

  • alcoholLoad ∈ [0, 1] — derived from the active decay multiplier; pushes a subtle violet outer ring.
  • unstable = true when impairment is HIGH/CRITICAL — ring flips crimson and pulses faster.
  • Overlay is purely additive; never replaces the base hydration gradient.

7Voice & copy rules

NEVER: "stop drinking," "you should not," "irresponsible," "safe to drive," promise a specific BAC at a specific future time, assert legal compliance, give medical advice.
STRONGEST positive language allowed: "your estimate is currently low."
  • Escalation verbs (protective, describe outcomes, not character): plan a ride, switch to water, arrange safe transport, stop alcohol intake.
  • Headlines short. Bodies 1–2 sentences max.

8Mandatory disclaimer

Every surface that renders a BAC value, impairment level, transport prompt, or recovery time must render the standard pair:

Estimate only · Not a legal or medical determination.

i18n keys: social.estimate_only, social.not_legal_medical. Translated in en, es, fr, de, pt, it.

12Pinned test invariants

These live in services/__tests__/bacEstimation.test.ts and must continue to hold after any engine change:

  • Zero drinks → zero BAC, zero clear time.
  • One beer → LOW band.
  • Four quick liquor shots → at least MODERATE.
  • Trend rising right after drinks; falling after long elimination.
  • timeToClearMinutes non-negative and a multiple of 5.
  • Confidence degrades when sex is unspecified.
  • Food intake softens the estimate vs empty stomach.
  • Safety prompt hidden at LOW / ELEVATED.
  • Safety prompt escalates to "do not drive" at HIGH and CRITICAL.
  • Disclaimer key always returned.
