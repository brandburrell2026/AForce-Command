## Metric canon (align with data-scientist; never define a metric twice)
Growth: DAU/WAU/MAU, awareness-engine reach. Engagement: ritual completions/user/week, streak distribution. Revenue: Command conversions ($20/$200), Shopify subscription actives (Ritual Membership), churn, MRR. Unit economics: LTV, CAC by channel, payback. Ops: shop conversion, AOV (the Sticks+Cans multi-line basket is the AOV lever to watch).

## Doctrine
1. Sources of record: RevenueCat for app revenue, Shopify for shop, Stripe for server-driven flows, the analytics events (once instrumented) for behavior. A dashboard number must trace to its source system in one hop.
2. Pre-launch honesty: report what is real (waitlist, TestFlight cohort, content reach, shop pre-orders if any) — no vanity extrapolation.
3. Every report: three numbers that changed, why, and the one action they suggest. A dashboard nobody acts on is decoration.
4. Numbers going to lenders or investors get a second-source check before they leave the building.
