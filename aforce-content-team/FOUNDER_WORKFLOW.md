# FOUNDER WORKFLOW — How Brandon Operates the Content Department

The department runs on four commands. Everything else is a specialist tool you reach for when needed. The design goal: **you see decisions; the agents handle execution; the workflows coordinate; the database remembers; performance data teaches; the Brand Brain protects consistency.** The system reduces what reaches you — an empty decision queue is success, not silence.

```
SPECIALIST AGENTS → WORKFLOWS → CONTENT DATABASE → TODAY / APPROVALS
      → /content-war-room → /ceo-review → YOUR DECISIONS
      → LEARNING SYSTEM → BETTER FUTURE CONTENT
```

## The daily loop (5–15 minutes)

**1. Start of day — `/run-day`**
Generates the operational brief and refreshes `dashboard/TODAY.md`: today's posts, filming, editing, approvals waiting, trend opportunities (real inputs only), the top hook, live experiments, deadlines. Run it first; the other views read what it produces.

**2. Operating view — `/content-war-room`**
The whole department on one screen: today + this week, the full pipeline (IDEA → … → WINNER), all 19 agents' status (honest `NOT RUN` where true), campaign phase and asset progress, performance (real numbers or `NO VERIFIED DATA`), winners per the detection rules, double-down recommendations split evidence vs. hypothesis, risks, and a DO TODAY / DO NEXT / WAITING / BLOCKED list. Use it to *see*; it doesn't ask you to decide.

**3. Executive judgment — `/ceo-review`**
The decision interface. Ten-bullet summary, then every decision that genuinely needs you — grouped APPROVE / EDIT / REJECT / DEFER, each with why it matters, the recommendation, evidence, risk, and real (never manufactured) urgency — plus top content, open claims/compliance items, brand-alignment flags, and what the system thinks it's learned (labeled FACT / CEO PREFERENCE / OBSERVATION / HYPOTHESIS / RECOMMENDATION). It ends with a numbered queue you can answer in one message: *"1 approve, 2 approve, 3 edit — change X, 4 defer."* Your answers are recorded to the decision ledger, statuses update, and edits feed the voice-learning loop. **One edit never becomes a brand rule; 3+ consistent decisions become a candidate you explicitly confirm.**

## Creation, as needed

- `/hooks [topic]` — 20–50 rated openings
- `/script [concept]` — a full production-ready script
- `/founder-content [topic]` — your and Julius's episodes (canon + [SLOT]s you fill)
- `/ugc [product]` — creator briefs with claims guardrails
- `/campaign [objective]` — a complete campaign package
- `/repurpose [content]` — one piece → clips, hooks, captions, carousel, posts, email, podcast angles

## Performance, once real data exists

- `/analyze-content` — honest analysis of uploaded/connected metrics (missing data stays missing)
- `/double-down` — winner detection + 5–10 intelligent variations of what worked

## Weekly — `/run-week`

The full department cycle (Monday recommended): performance review → strategy → 50+ hooks → scored concepts → scripts → founder + UGC → briefs → one batch-filming list → the week's calendar → community plan → 3–5 experiments → MUST/SHOULD/OPTIONAL priorities. Output lands in `reports/`, and `/ceo-review` picks up anything needing you.

## What the system will never do

Publish anything without your APPROVED · fabricate metrics, testimonials, creators, or scientific claims · turn one of your edits into doctrine · convert an unsupported claim into an approved one · bury a compliance flag. When data doesn't exist, it says `NO VERIFIED DATA`; when an agent hasn't run, it says `NOT RUN`.

## Where things live (when you want to look deeper)

`dashboard/APPROVALS.md` (the review queue you act on) · `data/ceo_decisions.csv` (your decision ledger) · `learning/ceo_preferences.md` (patterns awaiting your confirmation) · `brand/` (the Brand Brain — changes only with your explicit confirmation) · `campaigns/hydration-sticks-launch/` (the September launch package) · `training/` (drop real material here; the voice engine learns from it).
