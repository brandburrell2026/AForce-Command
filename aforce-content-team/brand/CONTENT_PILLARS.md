# AFORCE CONTENT PILLARS

**Version:** 1.0 · 2026-08-19 · Starting allocations are [PREF — leadership starting framework]; performance data re-weights them via `workflows/performance-learning-loop.md`.

## The three levels (portfolio layer)

| Level | Share | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **ALWAYS-ON** | 45% | Daily attention. Education, training content, quick founder thoughts, product moments, FAQs, trend reactions. Produced frequently, lightweight. |
| **CAMPAIGN** | 30% | Concentrated attention around initiatives — launches, flavors, partnerships, retail, events, seasons. Lives in `campaigns/`. |
| **CULTURE** | 25% | Identity people want to belong to. Bigger than the sticks. Governed by `CULTURE_STRATEGY.md`, expressed through `CONTENT_FRANCHISES.md`. |

Every calendar entry carries a level. Every weekly plan reports the split.

## The twelve pillars (subject layer)

| # | Pillar | What it is | Default level | Mix* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Performance Education** | Teach people how to perform better — preparation, focus, routines, sleep, training principles | Always-On | 25% education total (with #2, #8) |
| 2 | **Hydration Education** | Make hydration interesting: sweat, electrolytes, timing, myths — always inside `CLAIMS.md` lanes | Always-On | ↑ |
| 3 | **AForce Product** | Why AForce exists: sticks, cans, flavors, formats, the Demand system, honest product talk | Campaign / Always-On | 15% |
| 4 | **Founder Journey** | Build in public: Brandon + Julius, decisions, rejections, wins, retail, manufacturing | Always-On / Culture | 15% |
| 5 | **Athletic Performance** | Training, sports, movement, competition prep | Always-On / Culture | within 20% ent./culture |
| 6 | **Lifestyle** | What the AForce life feels like: mornings, travel, desks, gym bags — the ritual in the wild | Always-On | 10% |
| 7 | **Motivation + Discipline** | Performance mentality without cliché quote-cards. Standards, not slogans | Culture | within 20% |
| 8 | **Ingredients** | What's inside and why — "nothing added without a reason" | Always-On | within education 25% |
| 9 | **Culture** | Sports, fitness, and cultural conversations AForce can credibly join | Culture | within 20% |
| 10 | **Community** | Customers, athletes, creators, real stories, comments-to-content | Always-On | 10% |
| 11 | **Behind The Brand** | Manufacturing, testing, packaging, meetings, launches, development | Campaign / Culture | within founder/product |
| 12 | **Entertainment** | Content people would watch even if they never bought — humor, spectacle, storytelling | Culture / Always-On | within 20% |

*Starting content mix [PREF]: **25% Education · 20% Entertainment/Culture · 15% Founder · 15% Product · 10% Lifestyle · 10% Community/UGC · 5% Direct response.** The account is never a digital billboard.

## Rules

1. A piece has **one primary pillar** (for the database) even when it touches several.
2. The Calendar Manager enforces spacing: no pillar dominates a 3-day window; see the no-repetition system in `workflows/weekly-content-engine.md` §No-Repetition.
3. Pillar performance is reviewed monthly; re-weighting proposals are AI RECOMMENDATIONS until leadership adopts them.
4. New pillars require leadership approval — twelve is the ceiling until data argues otherwise.
