# AFORCE CONTENT FRANCHISES

**Version:** 1.0 · 2026-08-19 · Status: all franchises are [HYP] — pilot 3 episodes, then review against the success metric (rules in `CULTURE_STRATEGY.md` §8). Names are original to AForce; several grow from canonical brand phrases, which is the strongest kind of ownership.

## F01 — THE MOMENT BEFORE  *(flagship · Culture)*
- **Purpose:** own the brand thesis on film — what real performers do in the final hour before their moment.
- **Audience:** all personas; hero reach vehicle.
- **Format:** 45–90s cinematic mini-doc (Reels/TikTok/Shorts); long-form cuts for YouTube.
- **Host:** subject-led; no presenter. VO or captions carry it.
- **Frequency:** 2×/month (production-heavy).
- **Hook style:** time-stamp cold open — "6:12 AM. Three hours before the fight of her life."
- **Visual identity:** cinematic black grade, natural sound, slow push-ins; Signal Red timestamp overlay in IBM Plex Mono.
- **Episodes:** the amateur fighter's last hour before weigh-in · the line cook before a 400-cover Saturday · the teacher before the first day of school.
- **Product role:** ambient — present in the ritual if genuinely there; never inserted.
- **Success metric:** completion rate + shares (brand-winner category).

## F03 — STATED PLAINLY  *(Product/Trust)*
- **Purpose:** radical transparency series — plain answers about what's in the product, what it costs, what we won't claim.
- **Audience:** P4 Everyday Performers + skeptics in comments.
- **Format:** 30–45s direct-to-camera; carousel/X-thread versions.
- **Host:** founder or formulation-literate team member.
- **Frequency:** 2×/month + reactive (answers hard comments).
- **Hook style:** the uncomfortable question, verbatim — "'Isn't this just expensive salt?' Fair question."
- **Visual identity:** white seamless, product on table, IBM Plex Mono lower-thirds; zero music gimmicks.
- **Episodes:** why we won't say "boosts immunity" · what 0g added sugar actually means · why sticks cost $59.99.
- **Product role:** featured — honestly.
- **Success metric:** saves + comment sentiment shift; conversion assist.

## F04 — NOTHING WITHOUT A REASON  *(Ingredients/Education)*
- **Purpose:** one ingredient per episode: what it does (established lane), why it's in.
- **Audience:** P1/P4; high save intent.
- **Format:** 45–60s explainer with overlay diagrams; YT Shorts priority (searchable).
- **Host:** Education agent scripts; founder or creator delivers.
- **Frequency:** weekly during launch; biweekly after.
- **Hook style:** myth/mistake — "Sodium isn't the villain your feed says it is."
- **Visual identity:** Paper background, black type, single-ingredient macro shots; NIH-source card at end.
- **Episodes:** sodium, the misunderstood one · potassium and the signal your muscles wait for · what "marine minerals" means, plainly.
- **Product role:** closing beat — "it's in the stick for a reason."
- **Success metric:** saves + search-driven views over 30 days.
