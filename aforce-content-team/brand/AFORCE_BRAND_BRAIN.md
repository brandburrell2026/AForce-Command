## 2. The voice

Nine properties. All content is checked against them (Agent 02).

1. **Premium.** Never cheap, gimmicky, desperate, spammy, or hard-selling. Specificity is the premium cue: 4:58 AM, pH 8.8, 250 members, 20 servings — precise numbers, never vague superlatives.
2. **Powerful.** Language of movement, strength, momentum, and standards. Verbs over adjectives.
3. **Confident.** AForce doesn't beg for attention. It earns it. No exclamation marks doing the work confidence should do. **[FACT]** The canonical brand surfaces contain zero exclamation points.
4. **Concise.** Short sentences. Strong statements. Minimal filler. The signature construction is the **two-beat reversal**: *"We don't chase performance. We prepare for it." / "Everyone performs. Not everyone prepares." / "Hydration is not a claim. It is a process."*
5. **Aspirational.** Talk to the person the customer wants to become — the prepared one. But honor where they are: preparation is available to anyone, today.
6. **Performance-driven.** Every piece connects to hydration, preparation, training, focus, recovery, ambition, or daily performance. Performance is not a profession — it's any moment when someone is counting on you.
7. **Culturally aware.** Fluent in modern fitness, sports, entrepreneurship, wellness, and lifestyle culture. Participates only where AForce has a credible angle.
8. **Intelligent.** Science that is understandable without sounding like a medical textbook — and never claims more than the evidence lane allows. *"Nothing added without a reason. Nothing claimed without a lane."*
9. **Human.** Written like a person with a standard, not a model with a word count. The banned-language list in `MESSAGING.md` §5 is absolute.

**[FACT]** Register notes from canonical copy: quiet authority ("The world is loud. Readiness is quiet."); concepts named with "The ___" (The Ritual, The Standard, The Signal, The Demand, The Moment Before); plain-talk transparency ("Stated Plainly"); fragments used deliberately for rhythm ("Not a tagline. A behavioral operating system.").

## 6. Claims discipline (summary — full detail in `CLAIMS.md`)

**[FACT]** AForce operates under DSHEA structure/function rules and the repo's compliance framework. Approved-claim bank lives in `CLAIMS.md`. Never: diagnose/treat/cure/prevent framing, disease claims, medical outcomes, invented science. Established physiology (e.g., electrolytes contribute to normal fluid balance) is separated from emerging evidence and from brand interpretation, always. When in doubt: flag, don't publish.

## 7. Content philosophy

**[PREF]** The hierarchy, in order, for every piece:

1. **Attention** — stop the scroll. But a hook is a promise; the content must keep it. AForce earns attention through craft and truth, never through bait that spends trust. (The company constitution's rule — trust over attention — applies to marketing too: a hook that the payoff can't honor is off-brand.)
2. **Interest** — create curiosity.
3. **Value** — teach, entertain, inspire, or reveal something.
4. **Brand** — connect the idea naturally to AForce.
5. **Action** — a reason to respond, follow, save, share, visit, or buy.

Never begin with the sale. **[PREF]** The account is not a digital billboard: direct-response content is capped at 5% of the mix.
