## 5. Banned language — absolute [PREF, enforced by Agents 02 and 18]

**AI-pattern phrases (auto-reject):** "In today's fast-paced world" · "Whether you're X or Y" · "Unlock your potential" · "Game-changing / game-changer" · "It's not just X, it's Y" · "Elevate your journey / elevate your routine" · "In conclusion" · "Look no further" · "Dive in / let's dive in" · "Take your ___ to the next level" · "Say goodbye to ___" · "The ultimate guide to" · "Fuel your greatness" · generic motivational filler.

**Generic wellness clichés:** "wellness journey" · "self-care Sunday" · "mind-body-soul" · "holistic lifestyle" · "glow up" · "detox" (also a claims problem) · "superfood" (claims-adjacent; use only with QA sign-off) · "hydration nation."

**Tone violations:** exclamation-mark enthusiasm; emoji clusters (max one purposeful emoji on platform-native copy, none on brand statements); ALL-CAPS hype; fake urgency ("LAST CHANCE!!"); begging ("please like and follow"); competitor-slamming.

**Claims violations:** see `CLAIMS.md` §4 — diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, deficiency, disorder, immune-boosting, detoxifying, medical outcomes. Never negotiable.

**Competitor language to avoid echoing:** "Just do it"-style imperatives about greatness (Nike), wings/adrenaline framing (Red Bull), "the sweat life" (Lululemon), unlock-your-data framing (WHOOP), "science-backed hydration multiplier" phrasing (Liquid I.V.), "foundational nutrition" (AG1). AForce speaks about **the moment before** — that is nobody else's sentence.

## 6. Voice mechanics — how AForce sentences are built [FACT — extracted from canonical copy]

1. **Two-beat reversal:** negate the expected, assert the standard. "We don't chase performance. We prepare for it."
2. **Short declaratives.** Average sentence under ~12 words on brand surfaces. Fragments allowed, deliberately.
3. **"The ___" naming.** Concepts get definite-article names: The Ritual, The Standard, The Signal, The Demand, The Moment Before.
4. **Precise numbers as premium cues:** 4:58 AM, pH 8.8, 250, 20 servings. Never "tons," "super," "insanely."
5. **Verbs carry the power:** prepare, hold, build, carry, enter, lock in. Adjectives are rationed.
6. **Claims in lanes:** physiological statements pair *what it does (established role)* with *why we include it* — never a promise. See science-page pattern in `training/website/`.
7. **Quiet close.** Endings land on a standard, not a plea. "When the moment begins, the decision has already been made."
