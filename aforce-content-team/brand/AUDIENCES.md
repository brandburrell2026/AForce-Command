# AFORCE AUDIENCES

**Version:** 1.0 · 2026-08-19 · Tags: [FACT] canonical · [HYP] hypothesis until validated by real audience data.

**[FACT]** The brand's own frame: *"Performance is not a profession. It is any moment when someone — or something — is counting on you."* Canonical arenas: founders, athletes, surgeons, performers, parents, leaders, creators, first responders, operators. **Different arenas. Same standard.**

The five content personas below translate those arenas into targetable audiences. All persona details beyond the canonical arena list are [HYP] until customer research or performance data confirms them — see `training/customer-language/` as it fills.

---

## P1 — The Competitor

Athletes and serious trainers: lifters, runners, fighters, team-sport players, student athletes. Trains on a schedule, competes against yesterday. Speaks in sessions, splits, PRs, game day.
**What they want:** an edge that's real, not gimmicky; recovery that keeps up with ambition.
**Content:** training culture, competition prep (48-hours-out), heat/sweat education, recovery, athlete stories.
**Platforms:** TikTok, IG Reels, YT Shorts. **Products:** sticks in the gym bag; Training/Competition/Heat demands.

## P2 — The Builder

Founders, entrepreneurs, ambitious professionals. Performance arena is the pitch, the launch, the negotiation, the 5 AM block before the family wakes.
**What they want:** composure and focus under pressure; systems, not hacks; a brand that takes building seriously.
**Content:** founder journey (Brandon/Julius), discipline, deep-work preparation, entrepreneurship culture.
**Platforms:** LinkedIn, X, YouTube long-form, IG. **Products:** sticks at the desk and in the carry-on; Deep Work/Travel demands.

## P3 — The Operator

People whose moments carry weight for others: surgeons, nurses, first responders, pilots, military, shift workers, coaches.
**What they want:** reliability, seriousness, zero hype. They can smell a gimmick a mile away.
**Content:** The Moment Before stories, composure, preparation rituals, quiet-readiness culture. Handle with respect — never use their professions as props.
**Platforms:** IG Reels, YouTube. **Products:** sticks on shift; Daily demand.

## P4 — The Everyday Performer

Parents, professionals, and wellness-engaged people who perform daily without an audience. The parent before the household wakes is a canonical brand image.
**What they want:** to feel that preparation makes them the person their people rely on; approachable science; no bro-culture gatekeeping.
**Content:** morning rituals, hydration education, lifestyle, first-action content, honest ingredient talk.
**Platforms:** IG (Reels + Stories), TikTok, YT Shorts. **Products:** Variety Pack entry; Daily demand; AutoPilot.

## P5 — The Founding Community

**[FACT]** The Founding 250 — invitation-based first community; Brickell event October 2026. Early customers, creators, and believers who define what AForce becomes.
**What they want:** access, identity, and a stake in the story. They are members, not followers.
**Content:** behind the brand, build-in-public, first-look drops, community spotlights, event content.
**Platforms:** IG Stories, email/Substack, community surfaces. **Role:** first UGC engine and social proof.

---

## Persona rules

1. Every piece of content names its primary persona in the brief/calendar. "Everyone" is not an audience.
2. The voice never changes between personas — the **arena** changes. Same standard, different moment.
3. Never punch down: no mocking beginners, no elitism. Preparation is available to anyone, today.
4. As real audience data arrives (comments, buyer research, analytics), the Community Agent and Performance Analyst update this file — evidence-tagged.
