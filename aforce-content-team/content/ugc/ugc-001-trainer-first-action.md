# UGC-001 — First Action: The Trainer Who Opens the Gym

**Creator type:** Trainer (owns/opens a gym; real 5:30–6 AM reality). Cast via Agent 19.
**Platform / length:** TikTok + Reels · 15–25s. **Franchise:** F07 First Action. **Flavor:** Watermelon.

**THE ONE THING:** The person who prepares everyone else's session has a first action of their own — before the lights are even on.

**TALKING POINTS (their words, not ours):**
- What 5:40 AM at an empty gym actually feels like (the quiet before members arrive)
- Their real first completed action — keys, lights, water, the stick
- Why they don't skip it even on brutal days
- One honest line about coaching: you can't pour into people from empty

**WHAT NOT TO SAY (claims guardrails):** Fine: "part of how I stay on top of hydration," "my first action," "how I start ready." Never: cures/prevents anything, "boosts energy/immunity," medical terms, pH claims. **FTC:** #ad or platform paid-partnership tag — non-negotiable.

**HOOK OPTIONS (their register):**
1. "I open the gym at 5:40. Nobody preps the person who preps everyone else — so I do."
2. "This is not a morning routine video. I don't have time for one."
3. "The gym is empty for 20 minutes every morning. That's mine."

**SCRIPT BEATS (outline — do not fully script):** arrive dark → lights/keys sequence → the pour into whatever bottle they actually use → one sip, one line to camera → members start arriving → overlay "FIRST ACTION."

**B-ROLL:** key turn · lights banking on · empty floor wide · chalk bucket · front desk · first member at the door.
**PRODUCT INTEGRATION:** Stick + their real bottle; box visible in their bag, not staged on a shelf.
**CTA:** "What's your first action? Duet this."
**FORMAT SPECS:** 9:16 vertical, raw files delivered, natural audio; no music added by creator (we finish).
