# UGC-009 — Before the Household Wakes

**Creator type:** Parent creator (any configuration; the realer the better — toys on the floor welcome). Cast via Agent 19.
**Platform / length:** Reels · 20–35s. **Franchise:** F07 First Action. **Flavor:** creator's choice (any).

**THE ONE THING:** The canonical brand image made real: "the parent before the household wakes." Performance without an audience — the most relatable arena there is.

**TALKING POINTS:**
- The stolen half-hour: what they actually do with it (quiet coffee-adjacent honesty fine — water first, though, if that's true for them)
- Why the day goes differently when they get it vs. when they don't
- The moment the first kid-sound ends the quiet (humor welcome)
- One plain line: this is their version of showing up ready

**WHAT NOT TO SAY:** No supermom/superdad framing (punching down at un-prepared parents = brand violation); no health claims; kids' faces only with explicit consent (prefer feet/hands/door-crack silhouettes). FTC disclosure required.

**HOOK OPTIONS:**
1. "The house wakes up at 6:40. I wake up at 6:05. That 35 minutes is the whole trick."
2. "My arena has cartoons in it. The prep is still real."
3. "Before the household wakes — the least cinematic ritual on this app."

**SCRIPT BEATS:** dark hallway tiptoe → kitchen in half-light, toys visible (don't clean the set) → the ritual beat: glass, stick, slow sip against the counter → 60 seconds of something-for-them (stretch, list, stillness — whatever's real) → first kid-sound off-camera → smile, glass down, day begins → overlay "FIRST ACTION."

**B-ROLL:** monitor glow · tiny shoes by door · lunchboxes staged · sunrise through blinds.
**PRODUCT INTEGRATION:** The counter beat; box in a real cabinet among real groceries.
**CTA:** "Parents — what's your stolen half-hour look like?"
**FORMAT SPECS:** 9:16; ambient house-quiet audio; consent + privacy check per Agent 17 rules before featuring family.
