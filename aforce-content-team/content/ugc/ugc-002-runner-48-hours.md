# UGC-002 — 48 Hours Out: First Half-Marathon

**Creator type:** Runner (amateur, first or second half-marathon — the stakes must be real). Cast via Agent 19.
**Platform / length:** Reels + TikTok · 45–60s. **Franchise:** F06 48 Hours Out. **Flavor:** Berry. **Campaign:** hydration-sticks-launch.

**THE ONE THING:** The race is Sunday; the running is done. What the last 48 hours are actually for — sleep, fluids, logistics, nerves — through a real person's eyes.

**TALKING POINTS:**
- The weird feeling of NOT running two days out (the taper is mental)
- Their actual checklist: bib pickup, kit layout, meals, early nights
- How they handle hydration across the 48 (their words — "staying ahead of it," not medical dosing)
- The honest nerves; what they tell themselves

**WHAT NOT TO SAY:** No performance promises ("this will PR me"), no cramp-prevention claims, no dosing advice to viewers ("you should drink X") — their own routine only, framed as personal. FTC disclosure required.

**HOOK OPTIONS:**
1. "48 hours from my first half and the hardest part is doing nothing."
2. "The race is Sunday. The work is already banked. This is what's left."
3. "Watch me NOT run for two days. It's harder than the training."

**SCRIPT BEATS:** T-48 overlay: last shakeout jog → kit flat-lay + bib → grocery/meal reality → stick-into-bottle beat during the day ("staying ahead of it") → T-24 early night → T-2h morning, quiet, laces → walk to corral → cut BEFORE the start gun (the franchise never shows the event).

**B-ROLL:** race packet · flat-lay · alarm set · corral crowd energy · shoes at the door.
**PRODUCT INTEGRATION:** Natural fluid-prep beats across the window; box in the race-week kit.
**CTA:** "Racing soon? What's your T-48 look like?"
**FORMAT SPECS:** 9:16, Plex-Mono-style countdown overlays applied by our edit; deliver raw with timestamps noted.
