# UGC-007 — Soursop First Taste, With Family

**Creator type:** Lifestyle creator with genuine connection to soursop (Caribbean, Latin American, Southeast Asian, or African food heritage) — authenticity is the entire play; casting is everything (Agent 19 + F14 rules).
**Platform / length:** TikTok + Reels · 30–45s. **Franchise:** F14 Soursop Season. **Flavor:** Soursop. **Campaign:** hydration-sticks-launch.

**THE ONE THING:** For millions of people soursop is childhood; for the rest of the country it's a mystery. One family bridges that gap on camera — warmth first, product second.

**TALKING POINTS:**
- What soursop means in their family (real memories: the fruit, who served it, when)
- The taste-test moment: an elder or family member tries the AForce version and judges it honestly — **their verdict stays in the cut even if mixed** (honesty is the credibility)
- Explaining the taste to the uninitiated, their way
- The fruit-doesn't-travel truth: why stateside soursop is precious

**WHAT NOT TO SAY:** No soursop health-benefit claims (folk-medicine territory is a compliance minefield — taste and culture ONLY); no "authentic recipe" implications about the product; no stick-formulation specifics (pending sign-off). FTC disclosure required.

**HOOK OPTIONS:**
1. "My abuela is about to judge a soursop drink. Pray for this brand." *(adapt kin term to the real family)*
2. "If you know soursop, you already know. If you don't — sit down."
3. "The fruit that never survives the trip north finally sent a stick."

**SCRIPT BEATS:** kitchen, family present → one memory told over prep (30 words max) → the pour → the elder's taste + unfiltered verdict (the moment) → family debate/laughter → creator's own read on the flavor → close: "a restrained tropical edge — their words, not mine" or the family's better line.

**B-ROLL:** the real fruit if sourceable · family hands · kitchen warmth · label CU.
**PRODUCT INTEGRATION:** Central but judged, not pitched — the family's honest verdict is the ad.
**CTA:** "Tell us your soursop memory. Wrong pronunciations forgiven."
**FORMAT SPECS:** 9:16; natural kitchen audio; subtitles for any non-English lines (keep the original audio — never dub over family).
