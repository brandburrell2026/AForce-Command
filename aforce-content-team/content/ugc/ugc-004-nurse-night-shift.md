# UGC-004 — My Arena Is 3 AM: Night-Shift Prep

**Creator type:** Nurse or EMT creator (P3 Operator) — handled with full respect; their profession is never a prop. Cast via Agent 19 with extra vetting.
**Platform / length:** Reels · 30–45s. **Franchise:** F05 Different Arenas / F07 crossover. **Flavor:** Soursop.

**THE ONE THING:** Some arenas run at 3 AM. Preparation looks different when your "game day" is a 12-hour night shift with other people counting on you.

**TALKING POINTS:**
- Their real pre-shift ritual (sleep strategy, meal, kit check, the drive in)
- The weight of being counted on — said plainly, not dramatized
- Where hydration actually fits a 12-hour shift (their routine, their words)
- What "showing up ready" means when the stakes aren't a scoreboard

**WHAT NOT TO SAY (extra strict):** Nothing implying the product supports clinical performance or patient outcomes; no scrubs-as-endorsement framing ("as a nurse, I recommend") — this is *their prep story*, not a professional recommendation; no medical claims of any kind; employer/institution not identified without written clearance. FTC disclosure required.

**HOOK OPTIONS:**
1. "My arena starts when yours is asleep."
2. "Prepping for a 12-hour night shift — the version nobody films."
3. "3 AM is somebody's game day. It's mine."

**SCRIPT BEATS:** afternoon blackout-curtain wake-up → quiet kitchen, the ritual beat (stick + water, unhurried) → kit/badge/shoes → the drive, city going dark as they go to work → hospital doors (exterior only) → title card "Different arenas. Same standard."

**B-ROLL:** watch check · coffee AND water (honesty — both exist) · badge clip · parking structure night.
**PRODUCT INTEGRATION:** One kitchen beat + stick in the shift bag. Ambient, dignified.
**CTA:** "To everyone whose arena runs at night — what's your prep?"
**FORMAT SPECS:** 9:16; no hospital interiors, no patients, no PHI-adjacent anything; legal review of final cut mandatory (Agent 18 + leadership).
