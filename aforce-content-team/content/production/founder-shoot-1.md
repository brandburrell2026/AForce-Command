## FILM 1 — FD-001 · "4:58" (60s · darkest light)

**Hook variations (say whichever feels true in the moment):**
1. "Before this company had a name, it had a time. 4:58." *(scripted winner)*
2. "AForce didn't start in a lab. It started with a pause."
3. "The best idea of my life showed up when I finally stopped moving."

**TELEPROMPTER VERSION (verbatim, ~150 words):**
> Before this company had a name, it had a time. 4:58.
> A few years back, everything slowed down. Meetings stopped. Travel stopped. And for the first time in years I got something I didn't know I was missing — stillness.
> So I started driving here before sunrise. Meditate. Sit with my thoughts. Walk the shoreline while the day started.
> And one morning the question just landed: what if hydration belonged at the beginning of performance — not after it?
> The athlete before the whistle. The surgeon before the incision. The parent before the house wakes up. Everybody has a before. Nobody was building for it.
> I stopped walking, called my brother, and said the sentence that started all of this. But that's the next video.
> The ritual starts with Pause. Not because it sounds good — because stillness is where this company came from.

**NATURAL TALKING-POINT CARD:**
- The time: 4:58. Before the name, there was the time.
- What stopped (meetings, travel) → what arrived: stillness
- The drives here: meditate, sit, walk the shoreline
- The question that landed: hydration at the *beginning* of performance
- The four people: athlete / surgeon / founder / parent — everybody has a before
- Tease the call: "that's the next video"
- Close: the ritual starts with Pause because stillness is where this started

**Shot list:** ① WS dark shoreline walking ② MCU walk-and-talk (bulk of it) ③ MCU static — the stop, facing camera (the four-people beat) ④ WS horizon first light (close lines). **Overlays:** Plex Mono "04:58" at open · "next: The Call" at 0:45.
