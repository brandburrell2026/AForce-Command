## SCRIPT (45s, vox-pop, gym-district sidewalk or park — real answers only; the beats below are structure, not lines to feed people)

[0:00 — interviewer, walking energy] "Name your non-negotiable. The one thing you never skip."
[0:04–0:36 — 6–8 real answers, each cut with its Signal-Red card "NON-NEGOTIABLE:" + their words verbatim. Expected *range* (not scripted): the 5 AM gym answer, a family one (calls mom Sundays), a craft one (10 pages before bed), a funny one (keep it — humor humanizes), and one quiet-heavy one (sobriety walk, prayer, therapy homework — handle with dignity, air only with consent).]
[0:22 — PATTERN INTERRUPT mid-montage: someone flips the question back: "What's yours?" Interviewer, caught, answers honestly. One beat.]
[0:37 — closing VO over faces montage] "Everyone performs. Not everyone prepares. The prepared ones all had an answer ready."
[0:42 — card:] "What's yours? Comments."
