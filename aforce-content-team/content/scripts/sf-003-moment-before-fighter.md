# SF-003 — The Moment Before: The Fighter's Last Hour

**CONTENT IDEA:** Franchise F01 pilot: cinematic mini-doc of an amateur fighter's final 60 minutes before a bout — the brand thesis on film. No pitch, no presenter.
**OBJECTIVE:** Brand (shares, watch time); establish the flagship culture franchise.
**TARGET AUDIENCE:** P1 Competitor + all (culture reach). **PLATFORM:** Reels (adapt: TikTok, Shorts). **VIDEO LENGTH:** 60s. **CONTENT PILLAR:** Athletic Performance · Level: Culture.

## SCRIPT (60s — VO sparse; the film carries it. Subject: real amateur fighter, cast via Agent 19; facts on screen must be her real facts)

[0:00 — black frame, Plex Mono timestamp "T-60:00"] Hook overlay: "She fights in 60 minutes. Watch what she does with them."
[0:04 — locker room, quiet. She wraps hands. Ambient sound only.]

[0:12 — T-45:00 — she sits, eyes closed. Stillness.]

[0:20 — T-30:00 — water/hydration beat: bottle prepped, deliberate sips. No label push.]

[0:48 — walkout begins; we DON'T show the fight.]
[0:52 — cut to black. Line card:] "The moment does not make you ready. It reveals whether you prepared."
[0:57 — end card: N–N monogram. "THE MOMENT BEFORE — 001"]
