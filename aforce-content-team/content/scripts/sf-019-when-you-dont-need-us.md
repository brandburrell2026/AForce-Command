# SF-019 — Honest Answer: When You Don't Need Us

**CONTENT IDEA:** The ultimate Stated Plainly trust play: tell people plainly when they *don't* need electrolyte hydration. Under-claiming as the sharpest differentiation in a category built on overclaiming.
**OBJECTIVE:** Trust (saves, follows, sentiment); long-term conversion by credibility.
**TARGET AUDIENCE:** P4 + skeptics. **PLATFORM:** TikTok (adapt: Shorts). **VIDEO LENGTH:** 45s. **CONTENT PILLAR:** AForce Product · Level: Always-On.

## HOOK OPTIONS
1. [confession] "Honest answer: no, you don't need electrolytes for a 20-minute walk." — 9·9·9·10 = 37/40
2. [pattern interrupt] "A hydration brand telling you when NOT to buy hydration. Rolling." — 8·9·8·9 = 34
3. [contrarian] "Sometimes water is enough. There, we said it." — 8·8·9·9 = 34

**WINNING HOOK:** #1 — a brand voluntarily shrinking its own market is the rarest content on the feed.

## SCRIPT (45s, founder or team, direct, plain set)

[0:00] "Honest answer: no, you don't need electrolytes for a 20-minute walk."
[0:06] "Easy day, mild weather, three meals with normal food? Your body's regulation plus plain water handles that beautifully. That's not a weakness in our product. That's your physiology working."
[0:17 — the turn] "Where replacement starts earning its keep: long or hard sessions. Heavy sweat. Heat. Travel days that wreck your intake. Doubles. The demands — that's why we named them."
[0:28 — PATTERN INTERRUPT: leans back] "Why would we tell you this?"
[0:31] "Because we'd rather be the brand you trust on the days it matters than the brand that sold you something for a Tuesday stroll. Trust converts better than fear. And it lasts longer."
[0:41 — CTA] "Follow for more answers like this. We'd rather lose a sale than a reader."
