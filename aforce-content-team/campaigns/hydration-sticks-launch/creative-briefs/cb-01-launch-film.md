## CREATIVE OBJECTIVE
Launch-day anthem: prove in 60 seconds that the ritual survives every context — the product's entire reason to exist, shown not claimed. Success = completion + shares + "Begin the ritual" clicks (measured per campaign plan; no targets invented).

## NARRATIVE
Five strangers in five arenas perform the same four beats — tear, pour, sip, walk into their moment — cut in accelerating synchronization. The world changes; the ritual doesn't. No dialogue. The film argues the brand thesis (*performance begins before the moment arrives*) purely through repetition and rhythm, then names the product once at the end.
