# CB-01 — "THE RITUAL TRAVELS" Launch Film

**CONCEPT:** The 60-second anthem for launch day (Sep 14). One ritual performed across five arenas in one continuous editing rhythm — kitchen, gym, airport, hospital parking lot, dorm — proving the same four verbs survive every context. The stick is the constant; the world changes around it.
**AUDIENCE:** All personas · Reels + YouTube hero placement; 30s cutdown for TikTok; 15s cutdown for Stories/paid.
**HOOK (visual):** Frame one — five hands, five different waters (glass, shaker, hotel cup, bottle, fountain), one synchronized tear. Overlay: "The ritual travels."

**LOCATION:** Five real locations, one production day + one travel pickup: (1) home kitchen at dawn, (2) gym floor, (3) airport gate (or convincing terminal-adjacent), (4) hospital/shift-work parking structure at dusk, (5) dorm room. Backups: office desk swaps for dorm.
**TALENT:** Five real people from the creator/community roster (Agent 19) — no actors playing professions they don't hold. Wardrobe: their own, palette-checked (no loud logos, no color clashes with Paper/Black/Red overlays).
**PRODUCT PLACEMENT:** One stick per scene, entering at the same beat in each (the tear), building the synchronization motif. Variety of flavors across scenes (Watermelon dawn, Berry desk/dorm, Soursop dusk — flavor identities per PRODUCTS.md).

**SHOT LIST**
1. 5× CU hands + stick tear (matched framing, locked-off) — the opening grid
2. 5× MS pour into that scene's water (matched motion, left-to-right)
3. 5× swirl/dissolve macro (one per flavor color note)
4. 5× the sip + the settle (each subject's real face, held)
5. 5× the walk into their moment (gym floor, jet bridge, hospital doors exterior, lecture hall, home office)
6. Closing: black frame → line card → product lineup single shot

**CAMERA:** Mirrorless minimum (hero placement); 9:16 native composition; 24fps, 60fps only for pour/swirl; natural light + one bounce; tripod for matched frames (the sync IS the edit).
**B-ROLL:** location establishers (2s each), water textures, timestamp details (alarm 4:58, boarding time, shift board, class schedule).

**TEXT OVERLAYS (IBM Plex Mono, Signal Red accents):** per-scene location+time stamps ("05:12 · KITCHEN", "17:40 · GATE B22"…) · final cards: "Pause. Hydrate. Lock-In. Perform." → "THE RITUAL TRAVELS" → "AFORCE HYDRATION STICKS — WATERMELON · SOURSOP · BERRY".
**EDITING STYLE:** Metronomic match-cutting on the shared beats (tear/pour/sip) accelerating gently; ambient sound from each world layered under one restrained percussive build; Cinematic Black grade with scene-native warmth breaking through.
**MUSIC DIRECTION:** Minimal percussive build, no lyrics, no epic-trailer brass; silence under the first grid (the five tears ARE the beat).
**LENGTH:** 60s hero · 30s and 15s cutdowns specified in edit notes.
**CTA:** End card only: "Begin the ritual — aforce.com" (launch day exception within DR rules).
**REFERENCES:** The brand's own manifesto-page rhythm; F01/F05 visual identities in `../../brand/CONTENT_FRANCHISES.md`. No competitor film references by policy.
**PRODUCTION SIMPLICITY:** 4/10 (hardest piece of the campaign — 5 locations, matched framing). Mitigation: lock the shot template on location 1, replicate exactly; airport scene is the schedule risk — shoot it first week of production (rec: week of Aug 24).
