## CTA strategy by phase

- **The Before:** soft only — "Follow the Standard," saves/sends, arena comments. No links. The restraint is strategic: attention banked, not spent.
- **The Reveal:** direct CTAs activate within the DR allocation — "Sticks are live — link in bio," "The Variety Pack is the way in." One CTA per piece, never stacked.
- **Proof:** medium — flavor debates, "show us your arena," save-for-travel; DR retargeting continues on SF-020 only.

## Banned angles for this campaign (beyond the global banned list)

- Scarcity/urgency theater ("selling out," countdown pressure beyond the honest launch-eve countdown).
- Competitor comparisons by name; "better than water" framing (water is never the villain — SF-019 exists for a reason).
- Any stick-formulation specifics (pH value, botanicals) — **pending sign-off**, per `PRODUCTS.md`.
- Health outcomes: launch pressure is exactly when claim-creep happens; QA runs stricter, not looser, this month.
