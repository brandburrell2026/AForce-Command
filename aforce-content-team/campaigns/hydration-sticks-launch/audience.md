# THE RITUAL TRAVELS — Audience

Personas per `../../brand/AUDIENCES.md`; campaign-specific notes below. All behavioral detail is [HYP] until launch data arrives.

## Primary

**P2 The Builder** — the stick's sharpest wedge. Travel weeks and desk blocks are where routines actually die; "TSA-simple" and "the ritual travels" speak directly to their lived friction. Reached via LinkedIn (founder arc), X, Reels. Phase 2–3 conversion driver: Deep Work/Travel demand framing.

**P1 The Competitor** — gym-bag native. Heat-week timing (September training) gives urgency without fear language. Reached via TikTok/Shorts education + 48 Hours Out and Quiet Hours culture. Conversion driver: Training/Competition/Heat demands.

## Secondary

**P4 The Everyday Performer** — Variety Pack entry buyer ("you don't know your flavor yet"). Reached via First Action, flavor content, Stated Plainly honesty formats. The soursop curiosity engine (SF-006, UGC-007) recruits from well outside fitness — the campaign's audience-expansion bet [HYP].

## Community

**P5 Founding 250** — first orders, first proof. Stories-first treatment: early access energy, first-look drops, their arenas featured. October's Brickell event inherits whatever community heat September builds.

Miracle-seekers (we under-claim — they'll bounce; let them), discount-hunters (no launch discounts planned by the system; pricing is leadership's call), and hype-culture accounts (the quiet register will read as boring to them — by design).
