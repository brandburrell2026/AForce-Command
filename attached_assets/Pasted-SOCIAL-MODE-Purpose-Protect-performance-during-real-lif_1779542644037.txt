SOCIAL MODE

Purpose
Protect performance during real life.

Five blocks only:

Block 1 — Signal
States: LOCKED IN, FLOW, RECOVERING, RECOVERY REQUIRED
Subtitle: Protected for tomorrow.
Inputs: Recovery Capacity, Water Cycles, Time, Environment
Output: One state at a time only.

Block 2 — Command
One action only. Never stack.
Examples:
Drink 12oz water within 45 min.
Complete 1 Water Cycle.
Morning Reset available.

Block 3 — Forecast
Rules:
Recovery Capacity above 70 AND Water Cycles 5 or more — Signal up.
Recovery Capacity 50 to 70 — Recovery stabilizing.
Recovery Capacity below 50 — Recovery correction needed.
Maximum improvement 15 points per hydration cycle.
Forecast cannot exceed prior 7-day peak by more than 10 percent until sufficient history exists.
Display: One sentence only. No charts. No numbers visible.

Block 4 — Session Memory
Trigger: Auto-generates when user opens app between 5am and 11am after active Social Mode session.
Shows: Date, Aura state, Signal state.
Example: LAST NIGHT — Signal protected. Morning readiness up.
Button: SAVE
Storage: Local device only. Private.

Block 5 — Crew
Maximum 3 rows. Invite by username only.
Shows name and current state only.
No comments. No likes. No feed.

Hidden Engine
Inputs: HydroScan, Water Cycles, Time, Activity, Heat, Humidity, Sleep optional, Wearables optional.
No alcohol-specific UI, scoring, or user-facing alcohol outputs.
Outputs: Signal state, Forecast sentence, Recovery Window, Hydration Demand.

Aura Rules
Recovery Capacity above 80 AND Water Cycles 6 or more — LOCKED IN
Recovery Capacity above 80 AND Water Cycles below 6 — UNSHAKEN
Recovery Capacity 60 to 79 trend stable or improving — FLOW
Recovery Capacity below 60 OR trend declining — RECOVERING
Session completed successfully — RECOVERED
One word only. Updates once per session.

State Atmosphere Rules
CALM — above 75, no heat stress, low activity. Slow pulse, soft teal.
FLOW — 60 to 75, active, hydration on track. Steady pulse, bright teal.
RECOVERY — 45 to 59, correction active. Medium pulse, amber.
PROTECT — below 45 OR missed 2 consecutive cycles. Urgent pulse, deep crimson.
Only changes: orb speed, color, background lighting. No extra UI.

Reset Button
Label: RESET TOMORROW
Animation: Orb closes over 1.5 seconds.
Haptic: Single soft pulse. Optional. No sound.

Compliance
Never show: BAC, intoxication, safe driving, legal estimates, drink counts.
Only show: Recovery, Forecast, Hydration, Performance.
Disclaimer on first activation only: "Social Mode provides estimated hydration and recovery guidance only. It is not a medical, diagnostic, safety, or impairment tool."
