Add a new AForce OS feature called “Cruise Mode” for cruise line employees and guests.

Goal:
Cruise Mode helps crew members and guests monitor hydration, heat exposure, alcohol impact, activity level, sleep disruption, and recovery while onboard a cruise ship.

Create this as a premium feature inside AForce OS, not a separate app.

Core user types:
1. Crew Members
   - Housekeeping
   - Food & Beverage
   - Entertainment staff
   - Deck crew
   - Spa/Fitness staff
   - Officers
2. Guests
   - Families
   - Athletes/fitness guests
   - Older travelers
   - Party/nightlife guests
   - Excursion guests

Main Cruise Mode screen:
Title: Cruise Mode
Subtitle: Hydration intelligence for life at sea.

Add these sections:

1. Live Hydration Score
   - 0–100 score
   - Status labels:
     - Optimized
     - Monitor
     - Dehydration Risk
     - Recovery Needed
   - Show AForce recommendation:
     “Drink 12–16 oz water + 1 AForce stick now.”

2. Ship Environment Factors
   Include:
   - Temperature
   - Humidity
   - Sun exposure
   - Deck exposure
   - Indoor/outdoor work status
   - Sea day vs port day
   - Excursion risk level

3. Crew Performance Mode
   For cruise employees, add:
   - Shift length
   - Role type
   - Steps/activity level
   - Sweat risk
   - Break reminders
   - Hydration cycle reminders
   - Fatigue risk
   - Heat stress warning

   Example command:
   “You are entering a high-risk dehydration window. Drink water now and complete one AForce hydration cycle before your next shift block.”

4. Guest Wellness Mode
   For guests, add:
   - Pool/deck time
   - Alcohol intake estimate
   - Excursion activity
   - Fitness activity
   - Sleep quality
   - Recovery status

   Example command:
   “You had elevated sun and alcohol exposure today. Complete a recovery hydration cycle before dinner.”

5. Alcohol + Sun Risk Layer
   Add a visual risk card that combines:
   - Alcohol drinks logged
   - Hours in sun
   - Steps/activity
   - Sleep disruption
   - Hydration score

   Risk levels:
   - Low
   - Moderate
   - High
   - Recovery Critical

6. Port Day / Excursion Mode
   Add a port-day checklist:
   - Hydrate before leaving ship
   - Bring AForce stick
   - Log outdoor activity
   - Monitor heat index
   - Recovery reminder after returning

7. Crew Dashboard
   Create an admin-style dashboard for cruise operators:
   - Aggregate crew hydration risk
   - Heat risk by department
   - Anonymous wellness trends
   - High-risk shift windows
   - Hydration compliance
   - AForce product usage

   Do not show private individual health data unless user explicitly opts in.

8. Guest Engagement / Rewards
   Add:
   - Hydration streaks
   - Cruise wellness badge
   - Excursion recovery badge
   - Deck day badge
   - QR scan to log AForce usage
   - Reorder / buy onboard CTA

9. Design Direction
   Make it feel premium, oceanic, Apple-level, and cruise-line ready.
   Use:
   - Deep navy background
   - Electric aqua accents
   - Soft glowing hydration orb
   - Premium cards
   - Clean icons
   - Smooth transitions
   - Mobile-first layout

10. Navigation
   Add Cruise Mode as a feature inside the existing AForce OS navigation.
   It should connect to:
   - Hydration Score
   - Autopilot
   - Social Mode
   - Heat Mode
   - Recovery Mode

11. Demo Data
   Add realistic cruise sample data:
   - Crew member: 9-hour F&B shift, 18,000 steps, high humidity, moderate dehydration risk
   - Guest: pool day, 3 alcoholic drinks, 11,000 steps, high sun exposure, recovery needed
   - Excursion guest: 4-hour walking tour, high heat index, hydration warning

12. Business Positioning
   Include small copy blocks:
   - “For cruise operators: reduce dehydration risk, improve crew performance, and elevate guest wellness.”
   - “For guests: enjoy more, recover faster, and stay ahead of dehydration at sea.”
   - “For crew: smarter hydration support during long shifts, heat exposure, and high-movement days.”

Make this feature feel like a serious enterprise wellness product that could be sold to Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Norwegian, Disney Cruise Line, Virgin Voyages, and luxury cruise brands.
