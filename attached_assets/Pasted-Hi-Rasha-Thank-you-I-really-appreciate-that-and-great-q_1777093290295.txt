At launch in June, we are starting with hydration sticks and drinks as our primary entry products. These will be sold through direct to consumer on Shopify and through Amazon, supported by targeted activations in New York City with gyms and fitness communities. Our initial customer base will be young professionals and fitness focused consumers who are already purchasing functional beverages.
Customer acquisition will be driven primarily through Meta and Google. Meta will serve as our core acquisition engine to reach both new and in market consumers, while Google will capture high intent users who are actively searching for hydration and electrolyte solutions. In parallel, we will layer in creators and influencers to drive awareness and early trial.
From there, we are implementing a structured funnel that moves customers from awareness to purchase. We drive traffic into a quiz that identifies hydration needs and captures email and SMS. From there, we guide users into a seven day hydration challenge using a starter bundle, which is designed to create immediate product experience and habit formation. This increases conversion and repeat purchase behavior.
Once customers are acquired, we focus on retention and revenue expansion through email, SMS, and retargeting, driving repeat purchases across hydration drinks, sticks, and canisters. On top of this, we introduce AForce OS as a subscription layer that increases lifetime value over time.
Revenue Clarity
Our revenue model is built on a clear and repeatable structure. We acquire customers through Meta and Google, convert them through our starter bundle and seven day hydration challenge, and drive repeat purchases throughout the year. With an average order value of $52 and expected purchase frequency of 5 to 7 times per year, this creates strong lifetime value per customer. This is supported by Amazon and retail expansion in the second half of the year, which drives additional volume, while AForce OS introduces a recurring subscription layer that further increases revenue and retention. This structure gives us a clear and defensible path to achieving our $3.2M revenue target in 2026.
