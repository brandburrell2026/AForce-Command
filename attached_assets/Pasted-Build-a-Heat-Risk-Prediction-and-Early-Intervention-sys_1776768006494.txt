Build a Heat Risk Prediction and Early Intervention system inside AForce OS.

IMPORTANT:
This system must NOT claim to diagnose heat stroke.
This system must NOT present itself as a medical device.
This system must act as an early warning and intervention engine that detects elevated heat risk patterns before a dangerous event may occur.

The system should identify rising physiological risk, classify urgency, and trigger immediate hydration and cooling actions.

CORE GOAL:
Detect when a user is trending toward heat stress, dehydration overload, or possible heat injury risk before full breakdown occurs.

SYSTEM NAME:
Heat Guard

CORE PRINCIPLE:
AForce OS monitors signals continuously and identifies dangerous heat patterns early.
It does not wait for the user to ask for help.

OUTPUT MUST FEEL:
- urgent
- authoritative
- precise
- fast
- non-medical but serious

IMPORTANT LANGUAGE RULES:
Never say:
- “You have heat stroke”
- “Diagnosed”
- “Medical emergency confirmed”

Always say:
- “Critical heat risk rising”
- “Heat stress trend detected”
- “Immediate cooling and hydration required”
- “You are trending toward dangerous heat overload”
- “Stop activity now and begin recovery protocol”

RISK SIGNALS TO MONITOR:
Create a heat risk engine that uses mock or real-time inputs from available sources.

Inputs may include:
- hydration score
- recent fluid intake
- time since last intake
- ambient temperature
- humidity
- heat index
- sun exposure
- activity intensity
- continuous active minutes
- heart rate trend
- heart rate recovery delay
- sweat rate estimate
- body weight
- recovery momentum
- symptom inputs
- dizziness
- headache
- nausea
- cramping
- chills
- confusion
- fatigue
- urine signal
- energy state
- sleep deficit
- previous heat events

CORE RISK ENGINE:
Create a weighted Heat Risk Score from 0 to 100.

Example structure:

heat_risk_score = (
  heat_index_load +
  hydration_deficit +
  activity_stress +
  heart_rate_strain +
  recovery_failure +
  symptom_risk +
  sleep_penalty +
  sweat_loss_estimate
)

Clamp final score:
0 to 100

RISK STATES:
Create 5 risk bands:

1. STABLE
0–24
Low heat concern
User can continue with maintenance hydration

2. ELEVATED
25–44
Moderate heat load rising
Increase hydration cadence and watch trend

3. WARNING
45–64
Heat stress pattern forming
Immediate hydration and recovery intervention required

4. HIGH RISK
65–84
Dangerous heat trend detected
Stop activity, cool down, hydrate aggressively, recheck fast

5. CRITICAL
85–100
Extreme heat injury risk rising
Immediate stop, active cooling, emergency escalation guidance

IMPORTANT:
Critical does NOT diagnose heat stroke.
It flags a severe risk pattern and escalates action.

HEAT RISK OUTPUT:
For each user state, output:
- heat_risk_score
- heat_risk_state
- trend direction
- urgency level
- intervention protocol
- cooldown timer
- recheck interval
- AI command
- visual alert style

COMMAND EXAMPLES:
Stable:
“Hydrate now and stay on cadence. Heat load is under control.”

Elevated:
“Drink 12 to 16 oz now. Heat stress is building. Recheck in 20 minutes.”

Warning:
“Stop and hydrate now. Cooling protocol starts immediately. Recheck in 10 minutes.”

High Risk:
“Stop activity now. Move to shade or cooling. Drink fluids immediately. Recheck in 5 minutes.”

Critical:
“Critical heat risk rising. Stop activity now. Begin rapid cooling and hydration immediately. Seek on site medical support if symptoms escalate.”

INTERVENTION LOGIC:
When heat risk rises, the system must trigger action instantly.

Possible intervention outputs:
- hydrate now
- take AForce now
- stop activity
- move to shade
- cool body immediately
- remove layers if appropriate
- shorten next work interval
- force recheck timer
- alert coach or staff in team mode
