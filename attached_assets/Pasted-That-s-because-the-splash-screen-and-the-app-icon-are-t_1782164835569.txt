That's because the splash *screen* and the app *icon* are two separate assets. You fixed the splash screen, but this green tile is the **app icon** (the launch/loading icon Expo Go shows, plus the home-screen icon) — it's a different file that hasn't been touched yet.

You need to replace the actual icon image files and point `app.json` at them. The icon won't change just from editing the splash component.

Two things to know so you don't get stuck:

**The icon is cached.** Even after Replit swaps the files, Expo Go often keeps showing the old icon until you fully close the project in Expo Go and reopen it (or clear Expo Go's cache). So if it still looks green right after, that's the cache, not a failed change.

**Replit may not produce a crisp 1024 icon on its own.** It can wire `app.json` and generate a basic mark, but app-icon edges matter.
