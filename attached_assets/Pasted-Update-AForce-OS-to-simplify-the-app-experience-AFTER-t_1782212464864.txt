PHASE 3 — DAILY COMMAND HELPER
------------------------------------------------------------

Create or refine a pure helper that derives the single Home command from existing user state.

Suggested file:
utils/homeCommand.ts

The helper should return:

{
  eyebrow: string;
  title: string;
  body: string;
  ctaLabel: string;
  actionType: "log_water" | "start_protocol" | "scan_drink" | "recover";
  priority: "water" | "recovery" | "protocol" | "scan";
}

Priority order:
1. Water / hydration correction
2. Recovery need
3. Today’s protocol
4. Scan drink / optional check-in

Rules:
- This helper must be read-only.
- Do not mutate score.
- Do not award points.
- Do not fabricate completed behavior.
- Do not recommend product before water.
- Do not create a new scoring system.
- Use existing score, hydration status, recovery state, protocol state, and scan state where available.

Example outputs:

When hydration is low:
eyebrow: "HYDRATION COMMAND"
title: "Hydrate now"
body: "Start with 16 oz water before anything else."
ctaLabel: "LOG WATER"
actionType: "log_water"
priority: "water"

When recovery is the main issue:
eyebrow: "RECOVERY COMMAND"
title: "Recover now"
body: "Bring your system back into range before pushing harder."
ctaLabel: "RECOVER NOW"
actionType: "recover"
priority: "recovery"

When protocol is available:
eyebrow: "TODAY’S PROTOCOL"
title: "Start your protocol"
body: "Follow the next step and keep your readiness protected."
ctaLabel: "START PROTOCOL"
actionType: "start_protocol"
priority: "protocol"

When scan is appropriate:
eyebrow: "CHECK-IN"
title: "Scan your drink"
body: "Confirm what you’re drinking before it affects your readiness."
ctaLabel: "SCAN DRINK"
actionType: "scan_drink"
priority: "scan"
