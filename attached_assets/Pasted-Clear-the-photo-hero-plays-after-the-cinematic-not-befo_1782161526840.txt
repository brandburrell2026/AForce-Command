Two things I made sure the prompt nails for this specific ordering:

The **handoff** — "fades in as the cinematic fades out, no black flash." When you chain two full-screen overlays, the most common ugly bug is a split-second of black between them. Calling it out forces Replit to crossfade the two surfaces instead of unmount-then-mount.

And the key behavioral difference: the cinematic **auto-dismisses** (it's a timed sequence with "tap to skip"), but the photo hero **waits** — it has no skip and no timer, because it's the decision screen. It should sit there until the user picks Get Started or Sign In. The prompt makes that explicit so Replit doesn't accidentally auto-advance past your front door.

Same caveat as before: the placeholder image holds the layout, but this screen only becomes the WHOOP-killer when you drop in a real operator shot from your own footage. Judge the screenshot on scrim depth and wordmark legibility for now.
