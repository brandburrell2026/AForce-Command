AForce OS Home Screen Prompt

Build the AForce OS mobile app home screen inspired by the simplicity, premium feel, and focus of elite performance platforms like WHOOP, while creating a unique AForce identity.

Design Principles

* Minimalist luxury aesthetic
* Matte black background (#000000)
* White typography (#FFFFFF)
* AForce Red accent (#FF3B30)
* No clutter
* Large negative space
* Focus on performance, readiness, hydration, and behavior
* Premium athlete-grade experience
* Feels like Apple + WHOOP + On Running combined

When the user opens the app:

Splash Screen

Display centered AForce symbol/logo.

Animation:

* Fade in logo
* Subtle pulse effect
* Transition to home screen

Background:

* Pure black

No buttons.

Duration:

* 1.5–2 seconds
