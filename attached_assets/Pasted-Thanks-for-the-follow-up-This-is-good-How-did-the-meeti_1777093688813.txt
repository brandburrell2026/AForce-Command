Single product purchases on Amazon and retail equivalent pricing

Monthly or bi monthly usage patterns for hydration products

Benchmarks from comparable brands such as Liquid IV and LMNT

The addition of multiple product formats increasing usage occasions

Higher margin products such as hydration sticks and canisters

Lower margin but higher volume ready to drink and energy beverages

Direct to consumer mix improving overall margin profile over time

Paid social and creator driven acquisition across Meta and TikTok

Benchmarks from early stage beverage brands in the 40 to 70 dollar CAC range

Efficiency gains from organic, influencer, and Amazon driven acquisition

AOV multiplied by purchase frequency multiplied by gross margin

Repeat purchase behavior

Monthly pricing tiers of $5, $15, and bundled offerings
