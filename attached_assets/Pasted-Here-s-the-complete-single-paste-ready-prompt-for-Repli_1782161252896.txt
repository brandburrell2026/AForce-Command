The one thing that makes or breaks this is the **hero image**, and it's the part Replit can't supply — it'll drop in a placeholder. WHOOP's screen lands because that photo is a real, moody, expensive-feeling shot of an athlete in motion. Your version needs the same: an operator-class subject (athlete, surgeon, trader, founder) in low cinematic light, in their moment-before. That's exactly what your founder content shoots and the Brickell footage should produce — so the placeholder holds the layout now, and you swap in the real frame the moment you have it. The prompt is built so that swap is a one-file change without touching the layout.

When the screenshot comes back, check two things by eye: the **scrim is dark enough** that "GET STARTED / SIGN IN" read cleanly on near-black at the bottom (WHOOP's buttons sit on solid black — yours should too), and the **wordmark is legible** over whatever the placeholder image is. If either fails, it's a scrim-opacity tweak, not a rebuild.
