Update the AForce Hydration Score Algorithm so logged water and AForce products directly improve the user’s Hydration Score.

This is not a rewards point system.

Goal:
When a user logs water or an AForce product, it should positively impact the Hydration Score, Status Score color, AI Coach command, risk state, and recheck timer.

Create a hydration intake contribution model.

Inputs to add:
- waterOuncesLoggedToday
- waterOuncesLoggedLast2Hours
- waterOuncesLoggedLast4Hours
- aforceSticksLoggedToday
- aforceSticksLoggedLast2Hours
- aforceRTDsLoggedToday
- aforceRTDsLoggedLast2Hours
- aforceCanisterServingsToday
- aforceCanisterServingsLast2Hours
- lastWaterLogTime
- lastAForceLogTime

Hydration Score Positive Contributions:

WATER INTAKE
8 oz water logged within last 2 hours = +4 points
12 oz water logged within last 2 hours = +6 points
16 oz water logged within last 2 hours = +8 points
20 oz water logged within last 2 hours = +10 points
24 oz water logged within last 2 hours = +12 points
32 oz water logged within last 2 hours = +16 points

Water logged 2–4 hours ago should count at 50% value.
Water logged more than 4 hours ago should not boost current score.

AFORCE PRODUCT INTAKE
1 AForce Stick within last 2 hours = +12 points
1 AForce RTD within last 2 hours = +14 points
1 AForce Canister Serving within last 2 hours = +10 points
1 AForce Energy Drink within last 2 hours = +8 points

AForce products logged 2–4 hours ago should count at 50% value.
AForce products logged more than 4 hours ago should not boost current score.

HYDRATION CYCLE BONUS
If the user logs water and an AForce product within the same 30-minute window:
+8 bonus points

If the Hydration Cycle is completed during:
DECLINING state = +5 additional points
RISK state = +8 additional points
CRITICAL state = +12 additional points

CAPS
Maximum positive boost from water = +18 points
Maximum positive boost from AForce products = +18 points
Maximum positive boost from hydration cycle bonus = +15 points
Maximum total intake boost = +35 points

Important:
The score must never exceed 100.
The score must still account for negative risk factors like thirst, urine color, heat, activity, humidity, workout load, and time since hydration.

Scoring order:
1. Start with base score of 100
2. Subtract risk factors
3. Add water intake boost
4. Add AForce product boost
5. Add hydration cycle bonus
6. Clamp final score between 0 and 100

Create these functions:
calculateWaterBoost(inputs)
calculateAForceBoost(inputs)
calculateHydrationCycleBoost(inputs, currentStatus)
applyIntakeBoost(baseScoreAfterRisk, inputs)
getHydrationScore(inputs)

Return this updated object:

{

  totalIntakeBoost: number,
  lastWaterLogTime: string,
  lastAForceLogTime: string,
  nextEvaluationTime: string
}

Signal examples:

- “AForce intake confirmed”
- “Hydration cycle completed”
- “Recent fluid intake improving score”

Hydration Score 62 → 74

Show:
