==================================================
SCORING ENGINE
==================================================

Score range:
0–100 (clamped)

Add:
Water = +6
AForce = +10

Decay:

BaseDecay =
0.4 × (weight / 150) + (0.1 × activity_level)

HeatFactor =
max(0, (temp_C - 25) × 0.3)

HumidityFactor =
max(0, ((humidity - 50) / 10) × 0.2)

FinalDecayPerMinute =
BaseDecay + HeatFactor + HumidityFactor

Score formula:
Score(t) =
PreviousScore - (Decay × time) + inputs
