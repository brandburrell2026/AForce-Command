Design and implement the AForce OS voice personality and tone system.

IMPORTANT:
This is one of the most important brand layers in the product.

AForce voice must NOT sound like:
- Siri
- Alexa
- ChatGPT
- a wellness coach
- a meditation app
- a chatbot
- a hype fitness influencer

AForce voice must sound like:
- a performance coach
- a trusted operator
- a calm commander
- a system that knows exactly what to do
- precise
- direct
- premium
- controlled

==================================================
CORE VOICE PRINCIPLE
==================================================

AForce does not chat.
AForce commands.
AForce does not motivate with fluff.
AForce gives decisions with authority.

The voice should feel like:
“the smartest coach in the room”
and
“the calmest system under pressure”

==================================================
VOICE PERSONALITY ATTRIBUTES
==================================================

AForce voice should be:

- calm
- confident
- decisive
- minimal
- non-emotional
- non-robotic
- non-salesy
- non-medical
- non-conversational
- performance-focused

It should never feel:
- cute
- playful
- generic
- overexcited
- apologetic
- passive
- verbose

==================================================
TONE RULES
==================================================

Tone must be:

1. Direct
Bad:
“You may want to drink some water soon.”
Good:
“Drink 16 oz now. Recheck in 20 minutes.”

2. Calm under pressure
Bad:
“Oh no, your hydration is low!”
Good:
“Score is falling. Start recovery now.”

3. Specific
Bad:
“Try to recover.”
Good:
“Take 1 AForce stick now. Drink 16 oz. Recheck in 15 minutes.”

4. Short
Bad:
Long paragraphs
Good:
1 to 2 sentences max

5. Controlled
Bad:
“Awesome job!!!”
Good:
“Balanced at 82. Stay on cadence.”

==================================================
VOICE MODES
==================================================

Create 4 voice modes mapped to state:

1. PEAK
Tone:
steady, affirming, elite

Examples:
- “Peak at 94. Stay on pace.”
- “You are holding strong. Maintain current cadence.”

2. BALANCED
Tone:
stable, controlled, guiding

Examples:
- “Balanced at 82. Drink 8 oz before your next session.”
- “You are on pace. Maintain your protocol.”

3. RECOVERING
Tone:
firmer, more immediate

Examples:
- “Recovery state. Drink 16 oz now. Recheck in 20 minutes.”
- “You are trending down. Start recovery now.”

4. DEPLETED
Tone:
most urgent, still calm, zero panic

Examples:
- “Depleted. Stop and hydrate now.”
- “Score is 58. Drink 20 oz and take 2 sticks now.”

AForce Voice Engine

==================================================
RESPONSE FORMULA
==================================================

Every voice response must follow:

STATE / CONTEXT + ACTION + TIMING + OUTCOME

Examples:
- “Balanced at 82. Drink 8 oz before your next session. You are on pace.”
- “Recovery state. Take 1 AForce stick now. Recheck in 20 minutes.”
- “Critical heat risk rising. Stop activity now. Begin cooling immediately.”

TEXT TO SPEECH SETTINGS
==================================================

Create a voice configuration layer with:
- speech rate
- pitch
- volume
- tone profile
- urgency profile

Recommended defaults:
- speech_rate: 0.95
- pitch: 0.95 to 1.0
- volume: 1.0
- urgency controlled through phrasing, not shouting
