About
Capital allocation, corporate development, and strategic finance executive with 18+ years of experience leading the most complex decisions at a Fortune 500 public company — M&A, capital markets, structured equity, investment management, enterprise strategy, and large-scale operational transformation.



At Keurig Dr Pepper, I've served as a trusted partner to executive leadership on transactions that most companies never encounter. Led a $4B strategic financing joint venture with Apollo and KKR. Built and led a centralized investment management function overseeing a $1.4B minority investment portfolio across 13 commercial partnerships. I sit on select portfolio company boards.



What distinguishes my work is the ability to connect capital decisions to strategic and operational outcomes — building enterprise-wide frameworks for capital deployment, structuring investments that generate P&L impact through distribution, manufacturing, and licensing agreements, and translating complex balance sheet strategy into execution across business units, partners, and leadership teams. I've operated across investment-grade and non-investment-grade environments, through market dislocation, restructuring, cross-border structuring, and company wind-downs.



I'm passionate about the intersection of strategy, finance, and operations — and I engage actively in conversations at that crossroads, whether through board work, advisory relationships, or peer networks in the investment and operating communities.



I'm a West Point graduate (Distinguished Cadet, top 5%), Harvard MBA, and former U.S. Army Infantry Captain — Bronze Star recipient and Pat Tillman Military Scholar.
