Build a real-time Community Competition system inside AForce OS.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT social media.
This is NOT generic leaderboards.

This is a performance-driven competition system where users, cities, states, and teams compete based on real physiological performance and hydration behavior.

The system must feel:
- competitive
- real
- intense
- motivating
- premium
- like sports + culture

---

CORE CONCEPT:

AForce turns hydration into competition.

Users are not just tracking.
They are competing.

Competition types:
1. City vs City
2. State vs State
3. Teams vs Teams
4. Individual vs Individual

---

PRIMARY GOAL:

Make users want to win.

---

COMPETITION METRICS:

Rank users and groups based on:

- average performance score
- hydration compliance
- protocol completion rate
- recovery speed
- consistency (streaks)
- improvement over time

---

COMPETITION TYPES:

1. CITY VS CITY

Users are grouped by city.

Display:
- top cities ranked by performance
- city score (average of users)
- total participants
- momentum trend

Example:
New York: 88
Miami: 84
Los Angeles: 81

---

2. STATE VS STATE

Aggregate city data into state rankings.

Display:
- state leaderboard
- top performing state
- weekly winner
- monthly winner

---

3. TEAMS VS TEAMS

Teams can be:
- gyms
- sports teams
- friend groups
- companies

Each team has:
- roster
- team score
- team ranking
- coach view (future)

---

4. INDIVIDUAL COMPETITION

Display:
- global leaderboard
- friends leaderboard
- local leaderboard

---

UI REQUIREMENTS:

Create a new tab or section:
“Competition”

Inside include:

- leaderboard screen
- competition selector (city, state, team, individual)
- animated rankings
- user rank card
- team card
- city card

---

VISUAL DESIGN:

- dark premium theme
- bold rankings
- large numbers
- subtle animations for movement
- highlight top performers
- highlight user's position

---

RANKING ENGINE:

Create a scoring formula for competition:

competition_score = (
  avg_performance_score * 0.35 +
  compliance_rate * 0.25 +
  consistency_score * 0.20 +
  recovery_efficiency * 0.20
)
