Upgrade AForce OS Social Mode into a premium real-time alcohol mitigation, BAC estimation, and legal safety system.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT a party app.
This is NOT a bar game.
This is NOT a tool that encourages unsafe drinking.

This is a premium protection system designed to:
- reduce dehydration
- reduce hangover risk
- keep users aware of impairment risk
- guide hydration while drinking
- help users make safer decisions

The system must feel:
- elite
- controlled
- intelligent
- calm
- protective
- premium

It must NOT feel:
- playful
- reckless
- encouraging of overdrinking
- cheap nightlife software

==================================================
CORE PRODUCT DEFINITION
==================================================

Social Mode is a real-time alcohol mitigation and hydration protection layer inside AForce OS.

It should:
- track alcohol intake
- estimate hydration impact
- estimate impairment trend
- estimate BAC trend (approximate only)
- actively command hydration actions
- warn users when risk is rising
- transition into recovery mode after drinking

The system should help users:
- stay aware
- stay hydrated
- reduce next-day damage
- avoid unsafe choices

==================================================
CRITICAL SAFETY PRINCIPLE
==================================================

BAC estimation is approximate and informational only.

The system must NEVER:
- claim medical accuracy
- claim legal certainty
- tell someone they are safe to drive
- replace a breathalyzer
- act as a legal decision tool

The app must clearly state:
“BAC and impairment estimates are approximations only and must never be used to determine whether you are safe or legal to drive.”

==================================================
BAC ESTIMATION LAYER
==================================================

Build an estimated BAC engine using standard approximation logic for MVP.

Inputs:
- drink type
- drink count
- alcohol by volume (ABV)
- ounces per drink
- time since first drink
- time between drinks
- body weight
- sex at birth placeholder (optional if needed for model)
- food intake placeholder
- hydration compliance
- sleep state
- environmental heat stress
- activity level

Output:
- estimated BAC range
- trend direction
- confidence level
- time-to-clear estimate (rough)
- impairment risk level

Suggested output format:
- Estimated BAC: 0.06–0.08
- Trend: Rising
- Impairment Risk: Moderate
- Estimated Clear Time: 3 hr 20 min

IMPORTANT:
Always use ranges or approximation framing.
Do not present a single false-precision number as fact unless clearly labeled as estimated.

==================================================
BAC / IMPAIRMENT RISK STATES
==================================================

Create 5 safety states:

1. LOW
- light intake
- early drinking
- low impairment estimate

2. ELEVATED
- noticeable alcohol impact beginning
- hydration commands increase

3. MODERATE
- stronger impairment risk
- slow down warnings begin
- stronger hydration intervention

4. HIGH
- significant impairment risk
- stop drinking recommendation
- strong legal safety warning
- transportation guidance prompt

5. CRITICAL
- severe risk
- no more drinking command
- strong hydration / recovery command
- immediate transportation safety prompt

==================================================
LEGAL SAFETY LAYER
==================================================

Add a distinct legal safety and transportation protection layer.

This must:
- warn that estimated BAC is not legally reliable
- warn against driving after drinking
- recommend alternate transportation when risk is elevated
- provide ride option prompts when risk is high

Possible messages:
- “Do not use this estimate to decide whether to drive.”
- “Impairment risk is rising. Do not drive.”
- “Use a rideshare or trusted ride option.”
- “Your safest move is to stop drinking and avoid operating a vehicle.”

When BAC / impairment reaches MODERATE or higher:
Show a safety card with:
- transportation recommendation
- ride reminder
- legal disclaimer
- recovery command

At HIGH or CRITICAL:
Show stronger messaging:
- “Do not drive.”
- “Transportation required.”
- “Recovery protocol starts now.”

==================================================
SOCIAL MODE HYDRATION LOGIC
==================================================

Each drink increases hydration decay and raises hangover risk.

Core rule:
1 alcoholic drink must trigger a hydration action.

Examples:
- “Drink 1 water unit now.”
- “Take 1 AForce RTD now.”
- “Alternate with water before next drink.”

AForce RTD should be positioned as:
- faster recovery support
- hydration stabilization
- better next-step option than doing nothing

Do not be overly promotional.
Be useful and precise.
