01General Wellness Only

AFORCE OS, the AFORCE membership platform, our educational content, and features such as Readiness Scores are intended to support general wellness, healthy habits, and preparation. They are not medical products and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent any disease or health condition.

02AFORCE OS & Readiness Scores

AFORCE OS may present information, scores, streaks, and insights — including “Readiness Scores” — based on inputs you provide and, in the future, data from optional integrations. These outputs are informational estimates to help you build and maintain daily habits. They are not clinical measurements, diagnoses, or medical assessments, and should not be relied upon for any medical purpose.

Any potential future integrations (for example, Apple Health, Health Connect, Garmin, WHOOP, Fitbit, Oura, or Strava) are described as possible future functionality only and are not currently available. See the AFORCE OS Terms and Health Data Policy.

03Educational Content

Content we publish — including articles, science explainers, and habit guidance — is for general informational and educational purposes. It is not a substitute for professional advice tailored to your circumstances.

04No Medical Advice

AFORCE does not provide medical advice. Always seek the advice of a physician or other qualified health provider with any questions about a medical condition, hydration needs, supplements, or a fitness regimen. Never disregard or delay seeking professional advice because of something you read or saw in the Services. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your doctor or emergency services immediately.

05No Diagnosis or Treatment

Nothing in the Services is intended to diagnose a condition, recommend a treatment, or replace care from a licensed professional. Do not use AFORCE OS or its outputs to make medical decisions.

06Not a Medical Device

AFORCE OS is intended to qualify as a low-risk general wellness product and is not intended to be a medical device. It is not cleared or approved by the FDA and does not perform any regulated medical function.

You are responsible for how you use the Services and any decisions you make. Listen to your body, follow the guidance of your healthcare providers, and discontinue use of any product if you experience an adverse reaction.
