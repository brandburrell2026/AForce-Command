01Required Statement

These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

AFORCE products are foods and beverages intended to support hydration and general wellness as part of a healthy lifestyle. Individual results vary.

02Structure/Function Statements

Any statements we make about how AFORCE products or their ingredients may support the normal structure or function of the body (for example, supporting hydration or replenishing electrolytes) are “structure/function” statements. They describe general wellness support and are not claims to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent any disease or health condition.

03No Disease Claims

AFORCE does not represent that its products treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Nothing in our marketing, packaging, website, or the AFORCE OS should be interpreted as a disease claim. If you have a medical condition or specific dietary needs, consult a qualified healthcare professional before use.

04Product Classification & Compliance

AFORCE products are formulated, labeled, and marketed to comply with applicable U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) requirements for their product category, including conventional foods and beverages and, where applicable, dietary supplements under the Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act (DSHEA).
