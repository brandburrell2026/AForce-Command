The Founders—From the Beginning

Before the story was told.
Two brothers. One frame.

Julius Burrell — Co-Founder  ·  Brandon Burrell — Founder & CEO

01—The Stillness

The world slowed down.

04:58AM
Pause Before Possibility

Meetings stopped. Travel slowed. The constant noise was replaced by something Brandon had not experienced in years: stillness. Before sunrise, he began driving to Fort Lauderdale Beach. He would meditate, sit quietly with his thoughts and then walk the shoreline as the day began. Those mornings changed the direction of his life.

What if hydration belonged at the beginning of performance — not after it?

  • 01The athlete before the opening whistle.
  • 02The surgeon before the first incision.
  • 03The founder before the defining decision.
  • 04The parent before the household wakes.
That was the idea.
02—The Call—One Call, Two Brothers

Brandon stopped walking and called Julius.

“I think I just came up with the next big thing.”

Julius immediately understood what Brandon saw. This was not simply another alkaline beverage. It was an opportunity to give hydration a different role — to make it part of how people prepared. That phone call became the first day of AFORCE.

03—The Work

Then came two years of hearing the same answer.

“It’s impossible.”

Creating an alkaline hydration beverage with the taste, stability and performance profile they envisioned had not been done. Most people would have accepted the answer. The brothers treated it as the brief.

04—The Name in the File—Old File, Found
pH Science
01Open 02Call 03Connect

The spreadsheet that changed everything

The next answer was hiding in an old file.

While searching through his computer, Brandon found a spreadsheet he had not opened in years. On it was the name of a woman who specialized in pH science.

“I can’t do it — but I know the person who might.”

She introduced the brothers to an experienced beverage scientist whose work had supported nationally recognized products. He had never built anything exactly like AFORCE before.

“I’ve never built anything like this before. But I believe we can.”
05—Building What Didn’t Exist

Belief became repetition.

Formulate. Fail. Adjust. Return.

The process required repeated formulations, difficult decisions, long days and the discipline to keep going when the result was not ready. They were not trying to make hydration more complicated. They were giving it a clearer role: the first physical action in a performance ritual.

06—The Second Vision

Then the vision widened.

The drink was only the beginning.

As the beverage was taking shape, Julius had an unusually vivid dream. In it, AFORCE was not only a beverage company. Technology existed alongside the drink as part of one connected performance system. The product could create the first action. Technology could help people understand it, repeat it and turn preparation into daily behavior. When Julius shared the vision with Brandon, the next chapter of AFORCE began to take shape.

07—The Brotherhood—Behind the Discipline

The work was serious.
The brotherhood stayed human.

Unscripted — Between takes NN

AFORCE was not built by two people who agreed on everything. It was built by brothers who trusted each other’s strengths enough to challenge the idea, protect the standard and keep moving.

Brotherhood is accountability you cannot hide from.

Between the hard decisions, the setbacks and the pressure, they never lost the part that made the work worth doing together.
