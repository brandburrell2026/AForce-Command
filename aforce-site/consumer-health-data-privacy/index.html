Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This is a dedicated policy for consumer health data and is separate from, and supplemental to, our general Privacy Policy. It describes how AFORCE handles wellness and health-related data, including Apple Health / HealthKit-derived data used by the AFORCE OS app. Where a consumer-health-data privacy law (for example, a state “My Health My Data”-style statute) applies, this policy is intended to serve as the required consumer-health-data privacy disclosure.

Version0.1 (Draft) EffectivePending adoption PreparedJuly 7, 2026

01Scope

This policy applies to consumer health data that AFORCE collects, receives, or processes through the AFORCE OS mobile application and related membership features. It supplements our general Privacy Policy; where the two differ with respect to consumer health data, this policy governs.

“Consumer health data” means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to a consumer and that identifies a consumer’s past, present, or future physical or mental health status, as defined under applicable law.

[Pending confirmation — counsel]: confirm which consumer-health-data laws apply based on the go-to-market plan and user geography (for example, Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370, and comparable state statutes), and tailor the defined terms, rights, and timelines accordingly.

02Consumer Health Data We Collect

The AFORCE OS app is designed to work with wellness signals and logged behavior to present informational readiness and habit tracking. Categories of consumer health data may include:

  • Hydration and intake you log in the app;
  • Wellness signals derived from Apple Health / HealthKit that you choose to share, which may include heart rate variability (HRV), sleep duration, and resting heart rate;
  • App-generated indicators computed from the above (for example, readiness and recovery indicators). These are informational estimates, not clinical measurements.

This inventory must reflect verified product behavior, not marketing descriptions. The AFORCE marketing website (drinkaforce.com) does not itself collect consumer health data; the simulated values shown in website interface illustrations are sample data, not real user data.

03Sources of Consumer Health Data

  • You — data you enter or log directly in the app;
  • Apple Health / HealthKit — only the categories you explicitly authorize, on your device, through the operating system’s permission controls.

07Your Authorization and Consent

Where required by law, AFORCE will obtain your affirmative, opt-in consent before collecting or sharing consumer health data, separately from any general terms acceptance. Consent is not a condition of purchasing AFORCE products, and Apple Health / HealthKit access is optional.

[Pending confirmation — product/counsel]: confirm the in-app consent flow captures a durable record of consent (version, timestamp, and scope) and that separate authorization is obtained for any collection and any sharing, as applicable law requires.

08Apple Health / HealthKit Handling

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the AFORCE OS app requests access only to the specific data categories needed for the features you use, through Apple’s on-device permission controls. You can review and revoke this access at any time in the iOS Settings ’ Privacy & Security ’ Health controls, or within the app.

AFORCE handles HealthKit data in accordance with Apple’s requirements, including that HealthKit data is not used for advertising or other use-based data mining beyond providing health and fitness services, and is not disclosed to third parties for advertising or marketing.

10Access

Subject to applicable law, you may request to access the consumer health data AFORCE holds about you. See How to Exercise Your Rights.

Subject to applicable law, you may request deletion of your consumer health data. You may also delete data you logged, and disconnect Apple Health, within the app.

You may withdraw consent to the collection or sharing of consumer health data at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already occurred before withdrawal.
