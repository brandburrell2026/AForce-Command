Commitment

Accessibility

AFORCE wants everyone to be able to use our website and products. We are working to make our digital experiences usable and accessible, and we treat accessibility as ongoing work rather than a finished state.

On this page

01Our Commitment

We aim to align our website with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as a practical target, and we continue to identify and address barriers as part of our design and development process. Because our site and products are actively evolving, some areas may not yet fully meet that target. We do not claim that our site is fully conformant, “ADA compliant,” or “WCAG certified.”

02Request Assistance

If you have difficulty accessing any part of our website, or need information or a transaction in a different format, we want to help. Contact us using the details below and we will work with you to provide the information, product details, or support you need through an alternative method.

03Report a Barrier / Give Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please tell us. Helpful details include the page or URL, what you were trying to do, the barrier you hit, and the browser or assistive technology you were using. We review reports and use them to prioritize fixes.
