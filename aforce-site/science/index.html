Precision

Precision.

Engineered to perform. Built to last.

Not all beverages are manufactured the same. AFORCE ready-to-drink cans are produced using advanced thermal processing — retort — a controlled process chosen for stability, safety, and consistency from the first can to the last.

Why We Chose Retort Technology
01
Shelf Stability

No refrigeration required. Advanced thermal processing helps create a shelf-stable beverage that is ready whenever preparation is.

02
Safety

Every can is protected. Retort processing uses carefully controlled heat treatment to support microbiological safety.

03
Consistency

Every can performs the same. The process is designed to help deliver the same experience from batch to batch.

04
Convenience

Ready anywhere. Desk. Gym bag. Carry-on. Car. Studio. Firehouse. Flight deck. Preparation should travel with you.
