The idea

Every moment has a moment before it.

The lift. The pitch. The flight. The dinner. AFORCE OS is built for the minutes before them.

How AFORCE OS opens
01 · One Breath

Say it once.

Open your Moment, hold the red orb and say it in one breath. “Big presentation at 10. I want to be sharp, not rushed.”

02 · What I heard

See exactly what it heard.

Your words, word for word, and what AFORCE understood. Fix anything before it counts.

03 · Seal

Hold to seal.

The hours before the presentation, laid out, plus exactly what sealing will do. Nothing changes until you hold.

04 · Lock In Reminder you turn on · Preview

One reminder, if you want it.

Same member, another Moment: Thursday’s 7:00 PM dress rehearsal. Turn on a pre-Moment reminder and it arrives once, 30 minutes before. At the start, Lock In, and AFORCE goes quiet.

Off unless you turn it on. AFORCE doesn’t notify you after your Moment.

05 · Return

One question after.

The next time you open AFORCE after a Moment, like yesterday’s pickleball: did that plan work for you? Private. Answer it, or skip.

06 · Replay

You decide what it keeps.

Say what got in the way, like “Ran out of gas” after a morning lift. Nothing is kept unless you approve it, and you can edit, pause or delete it anytime.

07 · Next time

Your change, in the next plan.

Saturday’s morning run can reflect the change you approved for morning workouts, marked “Changed · from your Replay,” and why.

A kept change is your own experiment, not advice. Nothing changes unless you approve it.

Your Moments
