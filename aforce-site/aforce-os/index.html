01 / DESCRIBE

Say it once.

“Presentation tomorrow at 10. I want a little more time to get ready.” Review what AFORCE understood and edit anything before continuing.

02 / APPROVE

Make room before it.

Review an illustrative plan: materials at 9:15, a quiet reset at 9:45, presentation at 10. Seal what works. An optional pre-Moment reminder is a preview feature; the demo schedules nothing.

03 / REFLECT

Carry your choice forward.

When you return, you can reflect or skip. If you wanted more preparation time, approve an earlier start for your next presentation. You choose the change.
