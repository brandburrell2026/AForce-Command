# Post-Health-Connect Roadmap

**Founder-recorded 2026-08-19.** Sequence is binding; items must not be
reordered or started early without a new founder ruling.

1. **Google Health Connect + Samsung Health completion** — G4+G5 Android build,
   physical-device acceptance matrix (Samsung+Watch, Samsung w/o watch, Pixel,
   HC absent/outdated, full/partial/revoked grants, duplicates, WHOOP+Samsung
   simultaneous, offline, timezone, missing HRV/sleep).
2. **Freeze health-integration architecture** — after Google/Samsung
   acceptance, the canonical contracts, arbitration, provenance, dedupe and
   score firewall freeze exactly as verified.
3. **Full AForce premium UX/UI audit** — screen inventory first, then a
   Premium Experience Audit covering EVERY member-facing screen:
   *Current state · UX problem · visual problem · hierarchy problem ·
   interaction problem · premium opportunity · severity · recommended
   treatment.* **Do not begin until Google/Samsung is complete.**
4. **Premium redesign across every member-facing screen** — one reusable
   AForce premium design system is established BEFORE screen-by-screen
   implementation. No feature expansion during the premium pass. All verified
   backend behavior, HydroState math, health arbitration, provenance, scoring,
   persistence, auth and integrations are preserved untouched.
5. **Real-device visual/interaction acceptance** of the premium pass.
6. **Strava integration** — only after premium acceptance.
7. **Oura integration** — after Strava.

**Explicit prohibition:** Strava and Oura are NOT started immediately after
Health Connect. The premium experience is the next priority once
Google/Samsung acceptance passes.
