## 3. Recommended principle

**Prefer factual architecture language over superiority language.**

| Instead of | Prefer |
|---|---|
| "the only company that…" | "architected to combine…" |
| "no competitor has…" | "we have not observed in competing products" |
| "first / most advanced" | a description of what the system actually does |
| an unsupported physiological ranking | a scientifically reviewed statement, or nothing |

Architecture claims are **verifiable from the system itself**. Superiority claims require market
evidence the repository does not contain.
