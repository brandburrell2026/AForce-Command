## Founder rulings required

None of the above dispositions are blocked on a ruling — every closeable truthfulness gap was closed without one. Two follow-on product questions surfaced during the work that go *beyond* "is this claim true" into "should the product behave differently," and are named here rather than guessed at:

1. **Should the `context` row's own score contribution (not just the `recency` row's disclosure) apply the same connected-platform activity floor?** Today `computeDecayPerMinute` floors `activityLevel` for decay purposes only; `calculateBaseScore`/`buildBreakdown`'s `context` modifier (heat/sweat/activity penalty) always uses the raw manual slider. This fix made the *inconsistency visible* (the recency hint now says when the floor fired) without changing which number wins — changing what the `context` delta itself computes would be a scoring-behavior change, which this task's constraints explicitly route to a founder ruling rather than an engineering judgment call.
   - **Option A (recommended):** leave as-is. `context`'s manual-slider-only design may be intentional — the user's *reported* environment/effort (heat, sweat, activity) is arguably a distinct signal from *inferred* activity from a wearable, and conflating them risks double-counting activity's effect on the score (it already influences `context`, `outputStress`, AND now visibly influences `recency`'s rate).
   - **Option B:** float the `context` modifier's activity term through the same floor, so a heavy-step day also raises the visible heat/activity penalty, not just the invisible decay rate. This is a scoring-math change requiring re-verification of every `context`-dependent test and threshold.
2. **Should the Sleep pill itself be renamed/split** (e.g. "Sleep & Recovery," or separating overnight-carryover from platform signals into two pills) now that it's confirmed to fold in non-sleep platform signals, rather than only widening its copy?
   - **Option A (recommended, shipped this session):** keep the "Sleep" label and `DRIVER_MEMBERS` grouping (matches spec §8.2's four-driver naming), widen only the sentence copy to "sleep and recovery signals." Lowest risk, no spec change, closes the false-attribution gap.
   - **Option B:** rename the pill and/or split it into a fifth driver. Bigger UX change, requires a spec §8.2 revision and re-approval (the four-driver design is cited in code as spec-mandated), and was out of this task's unambiguous-fix authority.
